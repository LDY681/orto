<a href="https://datascience.uchicago.edu/people/madeleine-torcasso/">Madeleine Torcasso</a> is a postdoctoral researcher in the Department of Radiology at the University of Chicago.
She received her bachelor’s degree in Biomedical Engineering from Vanderbilt University in 2013 and her PhD in Biomedical Engineering from Texas A&M University in 2018.
She currently works with AI to quantify high-content optical microscopy images of human tissue samples.
She is also interested in using AI to merge data from multiple imaging modalities to improve predictive models, and is passionate about helping create open-source tools to help support researchers in producing reproducible results.
In her free time, she likes to get away from the computer and run, hike, or walk; anything to stay active!
