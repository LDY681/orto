The uveitides are a group of more than 30 diseases characterized by intraocular inflammation and are among the leading causes of blindness worldwide. They may result from infections, systemic inflammatory diseases, or immune-mediated processes confined to the eye, and are classified according to the primary site of inflammation. Progress in uveitis research has been limited by the lack of standardized diagnostic criteria and poor agreement among experts regarding disease classification. To address this challenge, research-focused classification criteria have been developed to improve diagnostic specificity and ensure consistent patient selection for clinical studies. The Standardization of Uveitis Nomenclature (SUN) Working Group, an international collaboration of 99 experts from 64 centers across 22 countries, was established to develop these standardized criteria.
