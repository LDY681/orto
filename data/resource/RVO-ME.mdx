The repository contains a manually annotated RVO-ME segmentation dataset.
The dataset comprises 3,012 OCT B-scan images from 130 patients, totalling 146 eyes.
Each image is annotated with segmentation labels for five critical retinal structures: subretinal fluid (SRF), intraretinal fluid (IRF), the ellipsoid zone (EZ), the external limiting membrane (ELM), and highly reflective foci (HF).
