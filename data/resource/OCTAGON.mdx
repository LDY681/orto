The OCTAGON dataset contains 213 retinal OCTA images, including 144 from healthy subjects and 69 from diabetic patients, acquired at both 3×3 mm and 6×6 mm fields of view and from superficial and deep retinal layers.
The images are typically 320×320 pixels and come with manual FAZ annotations provided by clinical experts (two annotations for healthy cases and one for diabetic cases).
The dataset is structured to support analysis across disease status, scan size, and retinal depth, making it particularly useful for benchmarking FAZ segmentation methods.
The images were collected with a Topcon DRI OCT. Díaz M, Novo J, Ortega M, Penedo M, Gómez-Ulla F. OCTAGON; 2018.
The dataset can be requested by contacting the data controller.
More information can be found on a VARPA group <a className="text-primary-400 underline" target="_blank" href="http://www.varpa.es/research/ophtalmology.html">website</a>.
