The computer-aided OCTA image processing system dataset is a large-scale retinal OCTA collection containing around 24,000 images from 3×3 mm and 6×6 mm scans.
It includes quality labels (good, moderate, poor) and is primarily designed for image quality assessment and FAZ segmentation labels tasks. Wang Y. et al.
A Deep learning-based quality assessment and segmentation system with a large-scale benchmark dataset for optical coherence tomographic angiography image analysis.
The article is available <a className="text-primary-400 underline" target="_blank" href="https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=4073651">[here]</a>,
the code is at <a className="text-primary-400 underline" target="_blank" href="https://github.com/shanzha09/COIPS">GitHub</a>,
and the dataset is at <a className="text-primary-400 underline" target="_blank" href="https://zenodo.org/records/5111975">Zenodo</a>.
