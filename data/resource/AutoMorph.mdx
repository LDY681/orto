**AutoMorph** - is a fully automated image analysis tool for retinal Fundus photography.
It consists of four modules: image preprocessing, image quality grading, anatomical segmentation (including binary vessel, artery/vein, and optic disc/cup segmentation), and vascular morphology feature measurement.
The tool is described in Zhou Y. et al. Automated retinal vascular morphology quantification via a deep learning pipeline. Transl Vis Sci Technol. 2022 Jul 8;11(7):12.
