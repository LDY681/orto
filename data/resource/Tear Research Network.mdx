The mission of the Tear Research Network is to standardize and harmonize tear fluid research, education and development. Only through these efforts can tear fluid biomarkers be effectively integrated into clinical practice, ultimately improving diagnostics and patient care. In addition, the Network aims to serve as a quality label for research studies, promoting rigor, standardization, and reliability in the field.
