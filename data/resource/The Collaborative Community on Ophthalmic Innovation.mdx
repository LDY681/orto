The Collaborative Community on Ophthalmic Imaging (CCOI) was established in 2019 to advance innovation in ophthalmic imaging by developing best practices, standards, and collaborative solutions. Its mission is to improve the diagnosis and management of eye diseases through multi-stakeholder collaboration.
