The ROSE dataset consists of three subsets designed for OCTA vessel and retinal structure analysis.
ROSE-1 includes 117 images from 39 subjects (mostly diseased), with superficial (SVC), deep (DVC), and combined vascular layers, split into training and testing sets and annotated at both pixel and centerline levels using expert consensus.
ROSE-2 contains 112 images (one per eye) focused on the superficial layer, acquired with a different device and manually traced by an ophthalmologist, with higher image resolution.
ROSE-O also includes 117 images and provides detailed annotations of retinal vessels, vessel junctions, and the foveal avascular zone (FAZ).
