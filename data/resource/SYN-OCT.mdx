Wong D., et al. (2026).
SYN-OCT: A synthetic dataset of ocular optical coherence tomography images from healthy and glaucoma eyes.
Scientific Data. Access via <a className="text-primary-400 underline" target="_blank" href="https://zenodo.org/records/17151869">ZENODO</a>.
The repository contains 200,000 images, comprised of 100,000 synthetic images from the generative glaucoma model and 100,000 synthetic images from the generative normal model.
Mean RNFL thicknesses and segmentation masks are also provided for each synthetic image.
