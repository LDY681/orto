Arikan M., et al. (2025).
OCT5k: A dataset of multi-disease and multi-graded annotations for retinal layers.
Access via <a className="text-primary-400 underline" target="_blank" href="https://rdr.ucl.ac.uk/articles/dataset/OCT5k_A_dataset_of_multi-disease_and_multi-graded_annotations_for_retinal_layers/22128671/4?file=44436359">University College London</a>.
The repository provides 5,016 pixel-level labels for 1,672 OCT scans spanning two diseases and healthy subjects, as well as 4,698 bounding boxes across 566 scans covering 9 biomarker classes.
Variations in retinal structure and imaging conditions make generalizable segmentation and detection difficult, requiring high-quality annotations.
Existing public datasets are limited in size, scope, and grading.
To address this, the authors introduce a comprehensive multi-grader, multi-disease dataset comprising three subsets (AMD, DME, and healthy) that support semantic segmentation and object detection.
